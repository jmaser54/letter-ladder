Letter Ladder

Created by Jeff Maser

How to Play

Build a ladder from the bottom to the top. Each rung adds exactly one letter to the word below it — you can rearrange the letters however you like, as long as every letter carries up plus the new one.

  • Words can be guessed in any order
  • NOT could climb to TONE, NOTE, or TORN
  • A letter can repeat — LEAD can become LADLE by adding a second "L"
  • Every word in the ladder must be a real English word
  • You can replace a word anytime by guessing another word of the same length

There's usually more than one path to the top — how many can you find?
